Why join

The perks of belonging

Everything about Gaming Empire is built around one idea: nobody plays alone unless they want to.

PERK / 01

Pick your roles, find your squad

Head to the roles channel and select every game you play. Then just ping the role in general chat and see who's up — instant teammates, no waiting around.

PERK / 02

Every skill level welcome

We don't discriminate on gameplay. Whether you're carrying the lobby or still learning the ropes, you'll always be treated as a teammate first.

PERK / 03

A genuinely friendly community

Our members are quick to help — whether you're stuck on a boss fight, need a fourth for ranked, or just want to hang out and chat.

PERK / 04

Loyalty is rewarded

Stick around for a year and you'll unlock an exclusive veteran role, displayed on your profile — a permanent mark of being part of the founding community.
