Community Guidelines

Server Rules & Guidelines

Welcome to Gaming Empire! To keep this a safe, fun, and non-toxic environment for everyone, please follow these guidelines. Breaking them may result in a warning, mute, or ban.

⚠ Strictly an 18+ server
01

Be respectful and civil

No toxicity, bullying, or personal attacks. Respect others' choices and privacy the way you'd want your own respected. Treat all members with respect, regardless of their skill level or game choice.

02

No spamming

Keep messages and caps lock to a reasonable level. Do not advertise other servers or products without staff permission — you're welcome to promote your own content or streams in #promote.

03

No inappropriate content (NSFW)

Keep all channels SFW. Sexually explicit, overly graphic, or maliciously linked content is strictly prohibited. If you'd like to post NSFW content, grab the NSFW role from #pick-ya-roles and post there.

04

No cheats, hacks, or exploits

Not many people enjoy playing with — or against — a cheater. Keep it to yourself, or you will receive a permanent ban.

We only want & give good vibes

Don't @everyone. You can use @role to an extent — just don't abuse it.
